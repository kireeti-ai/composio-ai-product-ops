Page Concurrency
Page concurrency is the number of pages of your r-1 and r-1 mini jobs that Reducto processes at the same time for your account. Pages above the limit wait in a queue, and Reducto processes them as soon as capacity is available.

How Page Concurrency Works
Work above the cap waits. Reducto queues the extra work and runs it when other work completes. You see added latency, not a 429 .
Higher limits. If your tail latency increases at high volume, the cause is work that waits in the queue. Contact sales for higher limits.
The cap is fixed. The cap does not grow with your traffic.
Each model has its own cap. r-1 pages do not count against the r-1 mini cap, and r-1 mini pages do not count against the r-1 cap.
Each region has its own cap. Pages in the US region do not count against your EU cap.
Edge Rate Limits
Edge rate limits apply to every request at the ingress and protect against accidental floods. They cap requests per second , not concurrent work. Requests above the cap return 429 immediately. The SDKs retry 429 responses with exponential backoff automatically.
