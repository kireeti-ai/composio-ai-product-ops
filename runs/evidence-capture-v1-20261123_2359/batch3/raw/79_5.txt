Frequently Asked Questions
 Can I change my plan?
You always have the option to upgrade your plan to unlock additional capabilities for project, program, or portfolio management. You can also add or remove users, purchase more licenses, or cancel your subscription at any time. These actions are subject to terms at Smartsheet User Agreement. 
 Do you have a service availability guarantee?
Yes, for customers with Enterprise plans. These plans are covered by our Service Level Agreement.
 What forms of payment do you accept?
We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express) and ACH direct debit. For Enterprise subscriptions that meet a minimum fee, we offer invoicing for payment via credit card, check, ACH, or wire. Payment options may vary by billing country.

 Is there a version of Smartsheet for Government agencies? 
Yes! We have Smartsheet Gov. Learn more about the capabilities of Smartsheet Gov. For more information, contact Sales.
Please note: Smartsheet Gov customers who purchase through an authorized sales representative will remain on the legacy collaborator model. However, if they purchase online and agree to our terms and conditions, they will transition to the new user subscription model.
 Is Smartsheet available in regions other than the US?
Yes! We offer Smartsheet Regions. Learn more about Smartsheet Regions or contact our sales team.
