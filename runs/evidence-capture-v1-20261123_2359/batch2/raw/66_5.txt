How do the different Neo4j AuraDB plans compare?
Free
: Start with a free plan to learn and explore graph data.
Professional
: Build high-performance applications with a production-ready cloud database.
Business Critical
: Ideal for mission-critical applications requiring a highly available database, robust enterprise controls, and 24/7 support. Perfect for running production workloads in a fully managed, secure, and flexible environment where uninterrupted access and security are essential.
Virtual Dedicated Cloud
: Ideal for customers needing highly customized, dedicated infrastructure with Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) and specific networking requirements. It offers a highly available database, robust security controls, and premium 24/7 support.
For more details on AuraDB capabilities, visit 
Neo4j AuraDB.
How does Neo4j AuraDB Professional differ from the free plan?
The free plan places limits on the number of nodes and relationships. It is suitable for small projects, learning, and prototyping. In contrast, AuraDB Professional offers enhanced performance and best-effort support, making it ideal for building high-performance, production-ready applications.
Is there a free trial for the paid plans?
Neo4j provides free trials on the AuraDB Professional tier as part of the paid plans, allowing users to explore the features and benefits before committing. The AuraDB Professional trial is available only in certain geographies for now. Contact

 or 
sign up directly

AuraDB on AWS Marketplace

What are the differences between Neo4j graph analytics product offerings?
Neo4j offers several ways to run its 65+ graph algorithms for tasks like community detection, node similarity, and centrality. The right choice depends on where your data lives and how much of the infrastructure you want to manage.
Neo4j Aura Graph Analytics
 (fully managed, pay-as-you-go): runs algorithms in on-demand sessions, with no infrastructure to manage. It’s available in every Aura tier, from Free (at no cost) to Virtual Dedicated Cloud, with limits on session size, concurrent sessions, and maximum session duration that vary by tier. 
See the tier comparison.
AuraDB Professional Graph Analytics Plugin
 (fully managed, no extra charge): a built-in plugin that runs algorithms directly on your AuraDB Professional database. Best suited to experimentation and non-production use.
Neo4j Graph Analytics
 (self-managed): runs in a dedicated analytics environment separate from your database, so you can scale and manage it independently based on your analytics needs.
Neo4j Graph Data Science
 (self-managed): runs alongside your Neo4j database and shares its compute resources, so you deploy and manage both together.
Neo4j Graph Analytics for Snowflake
 (fully managed, pay-as-you-go via Snowflake Marketplace): deployed in your Snowflake environment, it runs algorithms on demand directly on data in your Snowflake tables. 
See pricing on Snowflake Marketplace.
