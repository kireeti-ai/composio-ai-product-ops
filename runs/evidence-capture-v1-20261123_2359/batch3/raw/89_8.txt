Ramp MCP connects AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor to your Ramp account. Each connection acts as the signed-in user, with that user's Ramp permissions.

## Connect

- **Claude:** Install Ramp from [Claude's directory↗](<https://claude.ai/directory/61bac03c-3f98-4b3c-affb-1b99533fa82c>), then sign in to Ramp.

- **ChatGPT:** Install Ramp from the [ChatGPT app directory↗](<https://chatgpt.com/apps/ramp/asdk_app_69250fb6281c819195b52a1556b0060c>).

- **Cursor:** [Add Ramp to Cursor](<cursor://anysphere.cursor-deeplink/mcp/install?name=ramp&config=eyJ1cmwiOiJodHRwczovL21jcC5yYW1wLmNvbS9tY3AifQ>) with one click.

- **Claude Code:**

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http ramp https://mcp.ramp.com/mcp
```

- **Codex:**

```bash
codex mcp add ramp --url https://mcp.ramp.com/mcp
```

- **Other clients:** Coding agents can also use [Ramp CLI](</developer-api/v1/cli>). For other MCP clients, gateways, and the sandbox, see [Connect to Ramp↗](<https://agents.ramp.com/docs/guides/connecting>).

When you sign in, choose **Full access** or **Read-only**. Connections expire after 30 days; reconnect to renew.

## Connection issues

- **"The app you are using is not currently supported"** means your client's redirect URI isn't allowlisted. [Request a redirect URI](</developer-api/v1/mcp-redirect-whitelist-request>).

- **You can't connect at all:** your Ramp role may not include access to AI tools. Ask your Admin; see [Manage connected AI tools↗](<https://support.ramp.com/managing-connected-ai-tools>).

For the full tool list and more troubleshooting, see [Connect to Ramp↗](<https://agents.ramp.com/docs/guides/connecting>) and the [Agent tools reference↗](<https://agents.ramp.com/docs/reference/agent-tools>).
