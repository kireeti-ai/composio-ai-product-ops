Otter's MCP Server connects your meeting data directly to AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT, bringing your meeting intelligence into your everyday AI workflows .
What Is MCP?
Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI assistants securely access external data sources. With Otter's MCP Server, your AI tools can:
Search your meeting transcripts across all time periods
Analyze patterns and themes across multiple meetings
Generate content using your actual meeting data
Surface insights without leaving your AI workflow
The key advantage: Your meeting knowledge becomes instantly accessible to AI assistants—no more manual copy-paste or context-switching.
Secure by Design
OAuth-authenticated with granular permissions
Your AI assistant can only access meetings you explicitly authorize
